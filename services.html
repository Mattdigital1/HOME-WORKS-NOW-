Panel Upgrade Indicators

Your panel may need upgrading if you experience any of these:

  • Breakers trip frequently or won't reset
  • Lights flicker when appliances turn on
  • You're adding an EV charger or hot tub
  • Panel is over 25 years old
  • Home insurance flagged your panel
  • You're adding a generator transfer switch
  • Not enough breaker slots for new circuits

Natural Gas vs. Propane for Generators

Both are excellent generator fuel options. Here's the quick comparison to help you choose:

Natural Gas

Unlimited supply via utility line. Lower cost per BTU. Best if your home already has gas service.

Liquid Propane (LP)

Available anywhere — no gas utility required. Tank on-site. Great for rural areas or homes without gas service.

We install both options as part of every generator installation.

How a Generac Installation Works

From your first call to final inspection, we manage every detail. Here's exactly what to expect when you choose Home Works Now.

1

Free On-Site Assessment

Permit Submission

County inspection is completed, we walk you through operation, maintenance basics, and register your generator with Generac to activate your warranty.

FAQ

Generator Installation Questions Answered

Common questions from Naples homeowners about Generac generator installation.

How much does a Generac generator installation cost in Naples, FL? +
Generac whole-home standby generator installations in Naples typically range from $5,000 to $15,000+ depending on generator size (kW), transfer switch type, gas line requirements, and any electrical panel work needed. We provide free on-site estimates with written quotes — no obligation, no surprises.
How long does a Generac generator installation take? +
Most residential Generac installations are completed in 1–2 days. However, total project timeline includes permit acquisition (typically 1–3 weeks in Collier County), so plan on 3–5 weeks from first estimate to powered system. We manage the entire process and keep you informed throughout.
What size generator do I need for my Naples home? +
Generator sizing depends on your home's square footage, number of HVAC units, and whether you want essential circuits or full whole-home coverage. Most Naples homes are well-served by 16kW–22kW units. We conduct a free load calculation during our on-site assessment and recommend the right size — never upselling more than you need.
Do I need a permit for a generator in Collier County? +
Yes — Generac standby generator installations require both electrical and mechanical permits in Collier County and most SW Florida municipalities. Home Works Now handles all permit applications, coordination, and final inspections as part of every installation. You don't need to do anything.
Does my home need natural gas for a Generac generator? +
No. Generac standby generators can run on either natural gas or liquid propane (LP). If your home already has a natural gas connection, we can extend the line to the generator. If not, we can install a propane tank and fuel line. Both options are reliable and eliminate the need to store gasoline.
Why should I use an authorized Generac dealer vs. any electrician? +
Installing through an unauthorized dealer or non-certified contractor can void your Generac factory warranty entirely. As an authorized dealer, we register every unit with Generac, our technicians are factory-certified, and we have access to the 7-year extended warranty program. It also ensures you get genuine Generac components and approved transfer switches.
What maintenance does a Generac generator need? +
Generac recommends annual maintenance including oil change, filter replacement, spark plug inspection, battery test, and full load test. Generac also performs automatic weekly self-tests. We offer annual maintenance plans and membership packages to keep your generator ready. Learn more on our Generator Service page.

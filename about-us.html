Our Story

Built in Naples. Built for Naples.

Home Works Now was founded in 2019 with a simple goal: give Southwest Florida homeowners one trusted contractor they could call for everything — generators, electrical, plumbing, and gas.

We watched too many homeowners get bounced between multiple contractors, each pointing fingers at the other when something went wrong. We knew there was a better way — and we built it.

Today we hold four Florida contractor licenses — Electrical, General Contractor, Plumbing, and CAC — and we're a Generac Authorized Dealer. Every job we take is handled entirely in-house by our own certified team.

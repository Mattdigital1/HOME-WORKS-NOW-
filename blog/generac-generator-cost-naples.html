How Much Does a Generac Generator Cost in Naples, FL?

Home Works Now April 2025 6 min read Naples, FL

One of the first questions Naples homeowners ask us is: "How much does a generator cost?" It's a fair question — and the honest answer is that total cost varies based on several factors including home size, generator model, fuel type, and whether any electrical work is needed. This guide gives you a realistic breakdown based on what we see every day installing generators across Collier County.

Quick answer: Most whole-home Generac installations in Naples, FL range from $6,500 to $15,000+ for the complete installed system — generator unit, transfer switch, gas line, electrical work, and permits. Average for a typical Naples home (2,000–3,000 sq ft): $8,000–$11,000.

What Goes Into the Total Cost?

A Generac generator installation is not just the generator itself. You're paying for a complete, code-compliant system that includes the unit, transfer switch, fuel supply, electrical work, permits, and professional labor. Here's how each component breaks down:

1. The Generator Unit

Generac home standby generators range in price based on output (kilowatts). Here are the most common units we install in Naples:

2. Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS)

An automatic transfer switch is required for every standby generator installation. It detects a power outage and automatically switches your home from utility power to generator power within 10 seconds. Transfer switches range from $800 to $2,500 depending on type (whole-home vs. load-managed) and your panel configuration.

3. Natural Gas or Propane Line

Your generator needs a dedicated fuel supply. If your home already has natural gas service, we extend the existing line to the generator location — typically $500 to $1,500 depending on distance and complexity. If you're using propane, you'll also need a properly sized LP tank — an additional $800 to $2,000+ depending on tank size and placement.

4. Electrical Work

Some homes need panel upgrades or additional wiring before the transfer switch can be installed. If your panel is already sufficient (100A+ with available capacity), this cost is minimal. If a panel upgrade is needed, add $1,500 to $4,000. Most Naples homes don't require this.

5. Permits & Inspections

Collier County and Lee County require electrical and mechanical permits for generator installations. Permit fees typically run $300 to $700. We include permit management in every installation — we pull the permits, schedule inspections, and handle all county coordination.

6. Installation Labor

Professional installation by a Generac-certified team typically runs $1,500 to $3,000 depending on complexity (distance from panel to generator, gas line routing, etc.).

What Affects Price the Most?

  • Generator size: More kilowatts = higher unit cost
  • Fuel type: Natural gas is typically cheaper upfront; propane adds tank cost
  • Distance from panel to generator: Longer runs mean more wiring and gas line materials
  • Panel capacity: If your panel needs upgrading, that adds cost
  • Transfer switch type: Whole-home ATS costs more than essential-circuit switches
  • HOA requirements: Some Naples communities require screening or specific placement

Why Cheaper Isn't Always Better

You'll find online ads for generator installation at prices that seem too good to be true — and they usually are. Installing through a non-authorized dealer or uncertified contractor voids your Generac factory warranty entirely, meaning a single post-storm repair could cost thousands out of pocket. Always verify your installer is a Generac Authorized Dealer.

Does a Generator Add Home Value in Naples?

Yes — and meaningfully so. Real estate agents across Collier County report that whole-home standby generators are a significant selling point in Naples' market. Many buyers specifically look for homes with existing generators, particularly in storm-prone coastal areas. Independent studies suggest a whole-home generator adds 3–5% to home resale value, which for Naples' average home price can represent real money.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is financing available for generator installation?

Yes. Generac and third-party financing options are available. Ask us about current financing programs when you get your estimate.

Can I use my homeowner's insurance?

Generator installation is generally not covered under standard homeowner's policies, as it's a home improvement rather than a repair. However, some Florida insurers offer discounts on premiums for homes with standby generators — check with your insurance agent.

How long does the installation take?

Most installations are completed in 1–2 days. Permit acquisition takes 1–3 weeks in Collier County.

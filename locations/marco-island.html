Why Marco Island Homeowners Need a Generator

As a barrier island, Marco Island is uniquely exposed to Gulf storms. Power outages after major storms can last a week or more, and evacuation orders can leave your home unattended. A Generac generator provides critical protection even when you're not there.

Marco Island-Specific Benefits

Vacant Home Protection

Generator keeps sump pumps, security, and climate control running during seasonal absences and storm evacuations.

Storm Surge Risk

Marco Island's barrier island position means storm surge can knock out power grid infrastructure for days after a storm passes.

Protect Your Investment

Marco Island properties are among the highest-value in Collier County. A generator is essential property protection.

Remote Monitoring

Generac's Mobile Link app lets you monitor your generator from anywhere — perfect for seasonal residents and snowbirds.
